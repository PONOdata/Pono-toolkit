Standing on shoulders

Pono Toolkit is a fork.

Lenovo Legion Toolkit is the foundation. Almost everything Pono Toolkit does, it inherits from that project. Here is the credit, the license, and where support should go.

Lenovo Legion Toolkit is the foundation. Bartosz Cichecki started the project. Kaguya and Dr. Skinner have carried it forward. Years of WMI reverse engineering, ACPI exploration, hardware compatibility work, and feature design done by them is the entirety of what every Pono Toolkit build inherits.

If Pono Toolkit is useful to you, the most direct way to support the foundation it stands on is to donate to upstream.
